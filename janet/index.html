The problem

Scheduling is where private aviation loses money.

Every empty leg, every deadhead, every mis-assigned crew is revenue leaking out of your operation — silently, every single day.

🛩️

Deadhead by default

Manual planners choose the obvious aircraft, not the optimal one. Repositioning costs compound across a fleet into millions.

🧩

One trip, one answer

Legacy tools model a trip as a single fixed itinerary. The real world has hundreds of viable combinations — most never get explored.

😵

Crew burnout

Complex duty rules, rotation fairness, and preferences are juggled by hand — producing costly mistakes and unhappy crews.

🐌

Hours to decide

By the time a plan is built, demand has moved. Schedules that take days are obsolete the moment they're printed.
